Privacy statement

This statement explains how BISML B.V. handles personal data you provide through the BISML website and our contact channels.

1. Who processes your data?

BISML B.V. is responsible for processing personal data received through this website. For privacy questions or to exercise your rights, contact us at info@bisml.nl.

2. What data do we process?

When you contact us, we may process the following data:

  • name;
  • organisation;
  • email address;
  • the subject and content of your message;
  • information you choose to include in your message.

3. Why do we process this data?

We use this data to answer your enquiry, prepare a conversation or quotation, maintain our services and manage our communications carefully.

4. What is the legal basis?

Depending on your enquiry, we process data because it is necessary to take steps at your request before entering into an agreement, to perform an agreement, to meet a legal obligation or on the basis of our legitimate interest in handling business contacts carefully.

5. How long do we retain data?

We do not retain personal data longer than necessary for the purpose for which it was received. Enquiries that do not lead to a relationship or agreement are deleted when further follow-up is no longer reasonably necessary. Data forming part of an agreement or administration is retained in line with the applicable statutory retention periods.

6. Who do we share data with?

We only share personal data where necessary for our services or when we are legally obliged to do so. This may include providers of hosting, email or IT support. Where necessary, we make appropriate arrangements with processors to protect personal data.

7. Transfers outside the EEA

If a service provider processes personal data outside the European Economic Area, we put appropriate safeguards in place where required by privacy law.

8. Your rights

You may ask us for access to, correction, deletion, restriction or portability of your personal data. You may also object to processing based on a legitimate interest. Contact us at info@bisml.nl to exercise these rights.

You may also lodge a complaint with the Dutch Data Protection Authority.

9. Security

We take appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect personal data against loss, unauthorised access, unwanted alteration and unlawful use.

10. Changes

We may update this privacy statement when our services or applicable law requires it. The most recent version is always available on this page.
