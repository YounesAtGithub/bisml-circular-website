Cookie statement

This statement explains which functional storage the BISML website uses and how you can manage your preferences.

1. What are cookies and similar techniques?

Cookies are small text files that a website can store on your device. Similar techniques, such as local storage, can remember a preference in your browser.

2. Functional language preference

The BISML website uses functional browser storage to remember your chosen language preference (Dutch or English). This preference helps the website show your preferred language on a later visit.

Storage name: bisml-language
Purpose: remember language preference
Category: functional
Retention: until you remove this browser storage yourself or change your language preference.

3. No marketing or tracking cookies

In its current version, the BISML website does not use advertising, marketing, social media or analytics tracking cookies. We therefore do not display a cookie banner for tracking purposes.

4. Future external services

If we later add external services that use non-functional cookies or similar tracking techniques, such as analytics, embedded video, mapping services or advertising tools, we will update this statement. Where consent is required, we will ask for it in advance.

5. Removing your preference

You can remove the stored language preference through your browser settings, for example by clearing website data or local storage for this website. The website can then use your browser language or a new choice again.

6. Contact

For questions about this cookie statement, contact us at info@bisml.nl. Read our privacy statement for more information about personal data.
